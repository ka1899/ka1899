## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 💳 Payment Protection Insurance Credit Default Model
Engineered **2,000+ behavioral time-series features** from 13 months of statements for **50K AmEx customers**, then benchmarked Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and LightGBM. Reached **0.957 test AUC** (0.934 with the latest 6 months withheld) and turned default probabilities into risk-based premiums that held a 58.9% loss ratio against a 60% target.
`Python` `LightGBM` `scikit-learn` `SHAP` `Time Series`

### 🌡️ Heat's Toll on Health
23 years of CDC data across 31 states with DLNM lag models, a Streamlit app, and a Groq-powered AI analyst. Found a **32% rise in hospitalizations** against a 16% rise in heat events.
`R` `Python` `Streamlit` `Groq`

### 🛍️ AI Retail Agent
An n8n workflow agent on Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) that answers questions over the UCI Online Retail dataset, backed by PostgreSQL in Docker.
`n8n` `Groq` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`

### 🌊 Where Texas Floods
Geospatial analysis of **~394K NFIP flood claims** in Texas (1978 to 2026), compared with Louisiana and Florida. Hurricane Harvey alone produced 23% of claims and 52% of dollars paid, and the coastal share of claims is sensitive to how the coast is drawn, which the notebook tests.
`Python` `Plotly` `OpenFEMA` `Geospatial`

### 🛂 Sponsor Signal
Chrome extension for international students reading job postings: shows what the posting says about visa sponsorship, with the exact quote, next to the employer's H-1B certification record from Department of Labor data. Built with a teammate for Product Management with AI. No legal advice, no sponsorship predictions.
`JavaScript` `Claude API` `DOL LCA data`

### 🏦 NFIP Claims Lakehouse *(planned)*
End-to-end pipeline on FEMA flood insurance data (OpenFEMA to Delta to a SQL warehouse to a dashboard), aimed at where losses concentrate, how payouts and closing times vary, and which segments look underpriced. Still in the planning stage.
`PySpark` `Delta Lake` `Snowflake` `SQL`
